Privacy policy

Shorts Studio and World in Whoa · effective November 16, 2026

In short

  • Shorts Studio is private software used by one person, the owner of the World in Whoa YouTube channel, on their own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.
  • With the owner's permission it reads the channel's own video statistics from YouTube, read-only. It never reads anything about the people who watch.
  • Nothing it reads is sold, shared or used for advertising. It is kept on the owner's computer; the owner's AI assistant reads the figures to plan the next videos (see below).

What it reads, and how

Shorts Studio uses the YouTube API Services. The channel owner signs in on Google's own page and grants read-only access ("View your YouTube account"). With that, it reads only the owner's own channel:

  • the channel's name, handle and subscriber count;
  • the list of the channel's videos;
  • for each video: its title, when it was uploaded, whether it is public, and its view, like and comment counts.

It does not read the text of comments, who watched, liked or commented, or any other information about viewers. It cannot upload, change or delete anything on YouTube.

What it is used for

To show the channel owner how each video is doing (what it gained in the last 24 hours, and its counts a week after it went up), and to help choose what to make next. Figures that are worked out from YouTube's counts, such as "gained in the last 24 hours", are labelled as such.

Where it is kept, and for how long

  • Only on the channel owner's own computer. Each night, the owner's AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic, which does Shorts Studio's overnight work) is shown the channel's figures to decide what to make next; Anthropic handles that under its own terms. It goes nowhere else.
  • Hourly readings are kept for 8 days. Each video's latest counts, and its counts at 7 days old, are kept while the owner's permission stands.
  • Every check (hourly while the tool is open, and nightly) confirms with Google that the permission still stands, as YouTube requires at least every 30 days.
  • When a video is deleted from the channel, everything kept about it is deleted at the next check.
  • When the owner disconnects YouTube in Shorts Studio, the permission is withdrawn at Google and everything read from YouTube is deleted at once. If the permission is removed another way (for example on Google's security page), everything read from YouTube is deleted within 30 days.

Sharing

None, apart from the owner's AI assistant described above. YouTube data is not sold, rented, given to anyone else, combined with data about people, or used for advertising or tracking.

This website

These pages use no cookies, no scripts, no analytics and no trackers. The company hosting them may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP addresses) under its own policy.

Google and YouTube

  • By using Shorts Studio you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can remove Shorts Studio's access at any time on Google's security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

If you watch World in Whoa

YouTube's own privacy policy covers your use of YouTube. Shorts Studio receives no information about you.

Changes

If what Shorts Studio reads or does changes (for example, uploading the channel's own videos for its owner), this page is updated first, with a new date at the top.
